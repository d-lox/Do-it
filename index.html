Welcome to Do It
  • Do It is a focus planner for students. Plan your day, stay on task and see your progress in one place.
  • Add tasks, mark them not done, half done or done, and set an optional deadline.
  • A colour calendar, streak, goal countdown and progress graph show how you are doing.
  • A full-screen clock, Pomodoro and lap timer keep your study sessions focused.
  • Meditation gives you breathing exercises, focus sessions and calm ambient sounds.
  • Gentle reminders arrive only while you still have tasks waiting.
  • Make it yours with themes, colours and adjustable liquid glass in Settings.
  • There is no account. Everything stays on this phone and is never uploaded.
